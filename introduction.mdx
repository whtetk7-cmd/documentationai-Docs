## Welcome to SMITH

SMITH gives you a starter documentation workspace you can shape around your product, team, and release process. It helps you organize pages, keep content current, and present answers that stay aligned with your product as it changes.

This template is designed to be generic and easy to customize. Use it as a foundation for product guides, internal docs, help centers, or developer documentation.

## How SMITH fits into your workflow

SMITH is useful when you want documentation that supports the product instead of sitting apart from it. You can treat it as a living workspace for writing, reviewing, and publishing content that evolves with your team.

Use it to reduce drift between product behavior and written guidance. When your docs are easy to update, your users get better answers and your team spends less time maintaining outdated pages.

## Who should use SMITH

SMITH works well for product teams, documentation leads, support teams, and developers who need a shared source of truth. It is especially useful when multiple people contribute to the same documentation set and you want a consistent structure.

It also suits teams that want a clean starting point. You can begin with this template, then adapt the pages, navigation, and content model to match your own product and audience.

## Suggested documentation structure

A simple structure makes it easier for readers to find what they need. Start with an introduction, add a quickstart or setup guide, then expand into deeper topics such as configuration, authentication, and release history.

You can also group content by audience. For example, public product docs can live beside internal notes, while advanced configuration and changelog pages stay easy to scan.
