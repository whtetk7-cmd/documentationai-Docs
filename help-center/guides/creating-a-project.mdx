## Quick Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Projects">
    In the sidebar, click **Projects**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'New Project'">
    Name your project and add a description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your docs">
    Add pages, sections, and invite collaborators as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
