---
title: Page Not Updating
description: Fix SMITH doc update issues.
---

## Symptoms

- Edits not appearing after save
- Cached version still shows

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh browser">
    Press `Ctrl+Shift+R` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+Shift+R` (Mac).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check sync status">
    Confirm the "Synced" badge at top right. No badge? Try reloading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear cache">
    Log out, clear browser cache, then log in again.
  </Step>
</Steps>
